About Simple Panchang

Simple Panchang gives you today's Hindu Vaidik Panchang for your exact location, with the Tithi, Nakshatra and auspicious and inauspicious timings in one clean view.

It is built to be fast, readable and free of clutter, so you can check the day in a few seconds.

What it shows
🌙
Panchang elements
Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana, Rashi, Chandrabal and Tarabal, calculated for your place and time.
⏱️
Muhurat and Choghadiya
Auspicious and inauspicious periods, with separate day and night Choghadiya.
🪔
Vrat, Tyohar and holidays
Upcoming fasts, festivals and public holidays in one list.
